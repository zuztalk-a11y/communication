Phone-to-phone audio message test

Use this page on both phones. On the sending phone, encode a short text message into two high-frequency tones. On the receiving phone, listen for those tones and decode the message.

Important limitations
  • This is an experiment, not secure or guaranteed inaudible messaging.
  • High-frequency output and microphone sensitivity vary by phone. 17–18 kHz may fail, or may be audible to some people.
  • Keep the phones close (about 10–30 cm) and test in a quiet room. Start with low volume.
  • Microphone access requires permission and may require this page to be served over HTTPS.

Tips if it doesn't work

  • Try 16,000 and 17,000 Hz if the phones cannot handle the higher frequencies. These may be audible.
  • Try the slow bit duration and place the phones closer together.
  • Turn off audio enhancements if your phone has them, and avoid playing other audio.
  • Some devices or browsers filter out these frequencies, so failure doesn't necessarily mean the idea is impossible.
